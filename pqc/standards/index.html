The Round 3 additional-signatures portfolio

Announced in NIST IR 8610 (May 2026) as nine candidates; eight remain. Round 3 was expected to run roughly two years, putting any resulting standard plausibly in the 2028–2030 window. Chapter 16 of the book covers all of this in full.

The regulatory calendar

The least likely part of this page to move, and the most likely to matter — it is what turns the mathematics into somebody's deadline. Note the ordering that recurs in all of them, derived rather than arbitrary: key establishment before signatures, because a session key recovered later decrypts traffic captured today.

InstrumentRequirement
US EO 14412 (Jun 2026)Federal systems: key establishment by 31 Dec 2030; signatures by 31 Dec 2031.
NSA CNSA 2.0New national-security-system acquisitions to support the suite from 1 Jan 2027; software and firmware signing first.
